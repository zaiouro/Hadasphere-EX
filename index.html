Guides

A little guidance for the newcomers.

💬

Joining the Discord

  1. Click the Join the Discord button above.
  2. If the link is expired, tell the developer.
  3. Read #rules first.
  4. Say hi in #introductions.
🐛

Reporting a Bug

  1. Go to the #feedback channel.
  2. Title it: [Bug] one sentence
  3. Include: where it happened, what you did, what you saw.
  4. Screenshots or recordings help a lot.
💡

Suggesting an Idea

  1. Search first — it may already be suggested.
  2. Title it: [Suggestion] one sentence
  3. Explain why you want it and how it could work.
  4. Keep it to one clear idea per post.
📢

Tracking Progress

  1. This page's Development Log is always up to date.
  2. #updates on Discord has the full details.
  3. Enable Discord notifications so you never miss a major update.
